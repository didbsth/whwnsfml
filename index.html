Selected Projects

Rehabilitation App Usability Improvement

Industry-academic collaboration with EXOSYSTEMS

Worked in a four-person team to evaluate a healthcare app supporting cognitive rehabilitation and muscle rehabilitation. I analyzed usability to develop proposed improvements and conducted user research to evaluate them.

Reviewed literature to identify older adults as the target users and benchmarked other healthcare apps for seniors. Applied design heuristics informed by cognitive psychology to revise the interface. Made key information more visible and increased text size. Rewrote exercise instructions to make them easier to understand.

Developed usage scenarios for the app’s core functions and recruited people undergoing stroke rehabilitation for in-depth interviews. Compared the existing screens with the proposed designs through A/B usability testing. Examined whether users could move to the next step easily and recognize the main icons and instructions. The partner company adopted the final recommendations.

Pet Taxi Booking Platform

Individual project, Hyundai Motor Company Softeer Bootcamp, July to August 2026

Used interviews and market research to examine the customer journey. Identified that customers were booking rides without enough advance information about a driver’s capabilities or vehicle.

New Features for a Cinema Brand’s App

Four-person service design project, Korea University Graduate School

Conducted target-user research to inform service planning and app screen design. Used SWOT analysis to assess the brand’s position within the cinema market. Selected film enthusiasts as the core audience based on how the brand’s strengths could address market opportunities.

Conducted in-depth interviews with the target audience and supplemented these with ethnographic observation. Also studied online film communities. Found that access to collectible merchandise depended on stock at individual cinema branches. Proposed an NFT-based digital merchandise archive in response. Also identified difficulty finding seats with unbooked adjacent seats and proposed a recommendation feature that considers adjacent-seat availability.
